Duvan Tellez-Castro is an Assistant Professor at Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas Faculty of Engineering. His research interests revolve around the data-driven analysis and control of nonlinear dynamical systems, often with methods from operator theory. His group addresses research problems guided by applications in autonomy and robotics, networked power systems, and water transport systems.
